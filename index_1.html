// FAQ

Questions people actually ask.

Is Reelza free to use?

Yes. Pasting a link and saving a video costs nothing, and you don't need to create an account.

Do I need to log in to my Instagram account?

No. Reelza only works with public posts, so no Instagram login is required or requested.

Can I download private posts or accounts?

No. Reelza can only fetch content from public posts. Private accounts and private posts aren't accessible, by design.

Will the video have a watermark added?

No. Reelza doesn't add any logo, watermark, or branding to the file. You get the video as it was posted.

Is it legal to download Instagram videos?

Downloading is intended for content you own, or for personal, offline viewing of public posts. The rights to any video still belong to the person who posted it — don't re-upload or redistribute someone else's work without their permission. Read our terms of use.

Does this work for Reels, IGTV, and regular video posts?

Yes — Reels, feed videos, and IGTV are all supported, as long as the post is public.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. Reelza runs in your mobile browser, so there's nothing to install on iOS or Android.

Do you store the videos I download?

No. Files pass through only long enough to hand them to your browser, then they're discarded. Nothing is kept on our servers. Details are in our privacy policy.

Why did my download fail?

The most common causes are a private account, a deleted post, or a link that was copied incorrectly. Double-check the link opens in a browser first, then try again.

Is there a limit on how many videos I can download?

No daily limit. Just avoid sending automated bulk requests, which can get your access temporarily rate-limited.
