Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

What we don't collect

Reelza doesn't ask for your Instagram username or password, doesn't require an account, and doesn't ask you to log in to anything.

What happens to the link you paste

When you paste a link and press the button, the link is sent to our server. Our server asks a third-party media-lookup service for the public file addresses of that post. We don't keep a history of the links people submit and we don't attach them to any personal identifier. Like most hosts, our hosting provider's standard request logs may record the address of a request, including the pasted link, for a short time.

What happens to the video file

When you press a download button, the file is streamed from Instagram's content servers through our server to your browser. It isn't saved, kept in a library, or made available to anyone else.

Basic technical logs

Like most websites, our server and host handle short-lived technical data such as your IP address and a timestamp. We use it for security and to limit automated bulk requests. We don't use it to build a profile of you.

Cookies

Reelza doesn't set cookies for tracking, analytics or advertising. If that ever changes, this page will be updated first.

Third parties

  • Google Fonts. The site's typefaces are loaded from Google's servers, so Google receives your IP address and browser details when a page loads.
  • Media-lookup service. The Instagram link you submit is passed to an external API provider so it can find the post's public files.
  • Instagram / Meta. Videos and photos are hosted on Instagram's servers. We don't control how Instagram handles data. See Instagram's Data Policy for details.
